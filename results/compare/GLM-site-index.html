GLM-5.3-Flash on 2× RTX 3090 + 32 GB DDR4 + one NVMe

A recipe (and the measurements behind it) for running GLM-5.3-Flash (EXL3 2.05 bpw, ~76 GB of routed experts, 288 experts × 42 MoE layers, native MTP) on a consumer box that cannot hold the model in VRAM or RAM.
